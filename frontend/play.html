Instructions

Game Rules

  • Heads-up No Limit Texas Hold'em (2 players).
  • Starting stack: 100 BB each hand.
  • Max 2 raises per betting street.
  • Preflop raises are in BB multiples. Postflop raises are pot fractions.
  • Postflop pot fractions are relative to the effective pot (pot + amount to call), not just the displayed pot.
  • Folding when checking is available is removed as a valid action.

How to Play

  • Click an action button to play. Greyed-out buttons are not legal right now.
  • f = fold, ch = check, c = call, a = all-in
  • Number buttons are raise sizes. Preflop: BB multiples. Postflop: pot fractions.
